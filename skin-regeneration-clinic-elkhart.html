Your guide through every treatment

What is a skin regeneration clinic?

A skin regeneration clinic offers treatments that support the skin's own renewal process. At Belle Allure, PRF treatments and PRF microneedling are the main options, planned by Katherine Clark, RN.

How many sessions will I need?

Plans are personal. Katherine recommends the number of sessions and the spacing based on your goals and skin.

What should I expect afterward?

Mild redness and tenderness are common, temporary effects that usually settle within a few days. Katherine reviews written aftercare with you before you leave.

Is there a free consultation?

Yes. Your first consultation with Katherine is free.

How it works

How PRF supports your skin renewal

PRF, or platelet-rich fibrin, is a concentrate made from your own blood. Katherine explains how it is prepared and applied during your visit, so you know what to expect before treatment begins.

PRF is often paired with microneedling or used under the eyes. Each plan is spaced to give your skin time to respond, and results are reviewed at follow-up visits rather than promised in advance.
