Questions about services in Elkhart

Common questions before your first visit

Where is Belle Allure Aesthetics and Wellness located?

Belle Allure is at 320 West Bristol Street in Elkhart, Indiana 46514. Call 574-735-1528 to book or ask for directions.

Do I need a consultation before a treatment?

Every plan begins with a consultation where your goals, skin or hair history, and current medications are reviewed. Your provider then explains which options fit your needs.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a list of current medications, past treatments, and any questions. You can complete the intake form online before your visit.

Which weight loss medications does Belle Allure offer?

GLP-1 weight loss medications are prescribed after a medical evaluation, with ongoing guidance from your provider.

Does Belle Allure welcome first-time injectable clients?

Yes. Katherine Clark, RN, overseen by a medical doctor, welcomes first-time injectable clients and explains each step before it happens, at a pace that feels comfortable.

Can I use my HSA for treatments?

HSA cards are accepted for qualifying medical treatments, including TMJ Therapy, headache treatment, and IV therapy when your provider prescribes it for a medical condition. Cosmetic treatments such as Botox for wrinkles and dermal fillers are paid for directly.

When do I pay for my treatment?

Payment is completed before your visit, so you can focus on your care when you arrive.

What are the office hours in Elkhart?

Monday to Wednesday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Thursday, 8:30 AM to 4:00 PM. Friday to Sunday, closed. Closed on all holidays.
