Common questions

Questions about our aesthetic clinic

Will I look overdone?

Katherine plans treatments to keep your natural look. You can start small and adjust at follow-up visits.

How much do aesthetic treatments cost?

Pricing depends on the treatment and the amount your plan needs. Microneedling starts as low as $350 and PRF under eye repair starts as low as $250. Katherine confirms the exact price at your free consultation.

Is there downtime?

Many treatments have little downtime. Mild redness, swelling, or tenderness can occur and usually settles within a few days. Katherine reviews aftercare with you first.

Who oversees my care?

Katherine Clark, RN, provides care under the oversight of a licensed medical doctor.
