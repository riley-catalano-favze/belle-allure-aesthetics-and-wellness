Services

Aesthetic and wellness services in Elkhart, Indiana

Explore wrinkle treatments, dermal fillers, PRF therapy, PRF facial microneedling, PRF under eye repair, TMJ therapy, headache treatment, hyperhidrosis treatment, hair restoration, IV therapy, and wellness and weight loss support. Each service explains what it does, who it suits, and how recovery works.

Injectables

Wrinkle treatments

Treatments for wrinkles, brow lift, lip flip, vertical lip lines, and lower face. Each plan is built after a full review of your goals, and a 2-week follow-up lets your provider fine-tune your results.

Injectables

Dermal fillers

Liquid facelift, cheek filler, and lip filler add volume and define contours. Your provider chooses the product and placement around your features and goals, so each plan is personal to you.

Regenerative

PRF therapy

PRF therapy uses platelet-rich fibrin prepared from your own blood. Your provider recommends the right PRF option after your consultation, whether that is facial microneedling or under eye repair.

Explore this treatment →

Regenerative

PRF facial microneedling

PRF microneedling uses platelet-rich fibrin prepared from your own blood to support collagen and skin quality. Your provider reviews your skin goals first and explains each step before treatment begins.

Explore this treatment →

Regenerative

PRF under eye repair

PRF under eye treatment uses platelet-rich fibrin prepared from your own blood to support the delicate skin around the eyes. Your provider explains each option before you decide.

Explore this treatment →

Jaw relief

TMJ therapy

Options for jaw tension and teeth grinding, including TMJ massage and masseter treatment. Your provider recommends the right approach after your consultation, based on your symptoms and goals.

Headache treatment

Tox injections that may relax the muscles behind tension-related headaches. Your consultation reviews your headache history and goals before a plan is made.

Hydration and vitamins

IV therapy

Hydration, vitamin, and immune blends, including Quench, Get Up and Go, Myers cocktail, and Tri Immune Boost. Each blend is chosen to match how you feel and what you want to support.

Medical aesthetics

Hyperhidrosis treatment

Treatment options for excessive sweating. Your provider reviews your symptoms, history, and goals at your consultation, then recommends the right approach for you.

Hair restoration

A non-surgical treatment that uses your own platelets, which may support fuller-looking hair. Your provider reviews your hair history and goals first.

Wellness

Wellness injections and weight medications

Wellness injections such as B12, GLP-1 weight loss medications prescribed after a medical evaluation, and a skinny shot. Each option comes with ongoing guidance from your provider.

Skin health

Acne medication

Acne medication options prescribed after a medical evaluation of your skin. Your provider reviews your skin history and goals at your consultation, then explains each option before you decide.

Your guide through every treatment

Nervous about needles? Here is how we make it comfortable.

Katherine is a Registered Nurse with 20+ years of medical experience, and she welcomes first-time injectable clients. Tell her how you feel at your consultation, and she will explain each step before it happens. You set the pace, so ask her to pause whenever you need a moment. Ask about comfort options at your visit.

How do I choose the right treatment for my goals?

Your consultation reviews your goals, skin history, and expectations. Your provider then explains which options fit your needs and why.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Bring a list of current medications, past treatments, and any questions. Complete your consent and health history forms online before your visit, or on arrival. Either way, we are ready for you.

What aftercare follows a treatment?

Every plan includes written aftercare instructions and scheduled check-ins with your care team.

Where is Belle Allure located?

Belle Allure is at 320 West Bristol Street in Elkhart, Indiana. Call 574-735-1528 to book or ask for directions.

Who will guide me through my treatment?

Katherine Clark, RN, is your guide through every treatment, from your first consultation to your final check-in. She explains each option clearly, so you can make every decision with confidence.

Have any questions?

Request a phone call from Katie, and she will reach out to answer your questions and help you choose the right next step.
