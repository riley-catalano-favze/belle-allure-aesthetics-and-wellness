What it is

A concentrate made from your own blood

Platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) is a natural concentrate prepared from a small sample of your blood. Once the sample is spun in a centrifuge, it separates into layers. The platelets, white blood cells, and fibrin that form the concentrate create a soft, natural matrix that carries your body's own healing components.

PRF is a second-generation platelet concentrate. Many clinicians describe its fibrin matrix as releasing growth factors gradually over time. Katherine explains what the current evidence shows and how it applies to your goals.

Common questions

Questions about PRF therapy

Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Yes. Your free consultation with Katherine Clark, RN, comes first. She reviews your goals, health history, and current medications, then explains which options fit your needs and what each one costs.

How is PRF prepared?

A small blood sample is drawn and spun in a centrifuge. The concentrate that forms is then used in your plan. Katherine walks you through each step.

How soon will I see results?

Results develop gradually over several weeks. Katherine reviews your progress at follow-up.

Is there downtime?

Mild redness or tenderness is a common, temporary effect that usually settles within a few days. Katherine reviews aftercare with you before you leave.

Who should have a health review first?

Katherine reviews each health history before treatment and may recommend a different option or a short delay when any of the following apply: blood-clotting or platelet disorders, anemia, blood thinners or medications that affect clotting, active infection or skin conditions at the treatment site, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or plans to become pregnant, or active cancer treatment.

Important information

About results

  • Results are personal. They depend on your health history, the treatment area, and how your body responds.
  • Client stories reflect one person's experience and are not a guarantee of results.
  • Katherine Clark, RN, works under the oversight of a licensed medical doctor.
  • Evidence for PRF in aesthetic use is still developing. Katherine will review the current evidence with you.

Before treatment

  • Katherine reviews your health history and current medications before any treatment.
  • Bring a current list of your medications and supplements to your consultation.
  • You sign informed consent in person with Katherine before treatment begins.
  • Katherine may recommend a different option or a short delay if you have clotting, platelet, or blood disorders, anemia, or take blood thinners.

After treatment

  • Katherine reviews written aftercare instructions with you before you leave the office.
  • Your aftercare instructions are also emailed to you after your appointment, and you receive a printed copy to take home.
  • Contact our office with any concerns after treatment.

Photos and pricing

  • All visits are by appointment. A card is kept on file to hold your appointment.
  • Services, hours, and pricing may change. Call 574-735-1528 or book an appointment online to confirm current availability.
