What it is

How microneedling works

Microneedling uses a device with very fine needles to create tiny, controlled channels in the skin. Your skin responds with its natural renewal process. Katherine explains the microneedling facial, the growth-factor treatment included, and how it fits your goals.

Who it helps
Adults who want a refreshed-looking complexion with smoother texture and a more even tone.
Your visit
A consultation, then a microneedling facial with Katherine.
Results
Many clients notice gradual improvement as their skin renews over the following weeks.

Common questions

Questions about PRF facial microneedling

What does a microneedling facial cost?

Microneedling starts as low as $350. Katherine confirms the exact price for your plan during your free consultation.

How many PRF facial microneedling sessions will I need?

Plans are personal. Katherine recommends the number of sessions and the spacing based on your goals and skin.

What should I expect after PRF facial microneedling?

Mild redness and tenderness are common, temporary effects that usually settle within a few days. Katherine reviews written aftercare with you before you leave.

How soon will I see results from PRF facial microneedling?

Many clients notice gradual improvement as their skin renews over several weeks. Katherine reviews your progress at follow-up.

Who should have a health review before PRF facial microneedling?

Katherine reviews your history before treatment. A different option or a short delay may be recommended for active infection or skin conditions at the treatment site, bleeding or clotting disorders, blood thinners, pregnancy or breastfeeding, or active cancer treatment.

Important information

About results

  • Results are personal. They depend on your health history, the treatment area, and how your body responds.
  • Client stories reflect one person's experience and are not a guarantee of results.
  • Katherine Clark, RN, works under the oversight of a licensed medical doctor.
  • Results vary. Katherine reviews the current evidence for microneedling and PRF with you during your consultation.

Before treatment

  • Katherine reviews your health history and current medications before any treatment.
  • Bring a current list of your medications and supplements to your consultation.
  • You sign informed consent in person with Katherine before treatment begins.

After treatment

  • Katherine reviews written aftercare instructions with you before you leave the office.
  • Your aftercare instructions are also emailed to you after your appointment, and you receive a printed copy to take home.
  • Contact our office with any concerns after treatment.

Photos and pricing

  • Photos of models are for illustration. Before and after photos show individual clients.
  • All visits are by appointment. A card is kept on file to hold your appointment.
  • Services, hours, and pricing may change. Call 574-735-1528 or book an appointment online to confirm current availability.
