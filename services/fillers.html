What it is

How dermal fillers work

Many dermal fillers are hyaluronic acid gels. They add volume and support the shape of the treated area. Katherine selects the product for each area and explains what it is designed to do.

Many hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme if you want a change, which gives you flexibility as your goals evolve.

Who it helps
Adults who want more volume, lift, or contour in specific areas.

Common questions

Questions about Dermal fillers

How long do fillers last?

Duration depends on the product and the area treated. Katherine reviews the expected timing for your plan during your consultation.

Is there downtime after filler?

Mild swelling, tenderness, or bruising can occur and usually settles over several days. Katherine reviews aftercare with you before you leave.

Can fillers be adjusted or reversed?

Many hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme if you want a change. Katherine explains which products she uses and how adjustments work.

Who should have a health review before dermal fillers?

Katherine reviews your history before treatment. A different option or a short delay may be recommended for an active infection at the treatment site, pregnancy or breastfeeding, bleeding disorders or blood thinners, or a history of severe allergic reactions.

How much does filler cost?

Pricing depends on the product and the amount your plan needs. Katherine confirms the exact price for your plan during your free consultation.

Important information

About results

  • Results are personal. They depend on your health history, the treatment area, and how your body responds.
  • Client stories reflect one person's experience and are not a guarantee of results.
  • Katherine Clark, RN, works under the oversight of a licensed medical doctor.
  • Dermal fillers are FDA-approved for specific areas and uses that vary by product. Katherine explains the approved uses of the product she recommends and any off-label use before treatment.

Before treatment

  • Katherine reviews your health history and current medications before any treatment.
  • Bring a current list of your medications and supplements to your consultation.
  • You sign informed consent in person with Katherine before treatment begins.

After treatment

  • Katherine reviews written aftercare instructions with you before you leave the office.
  • Your aftercare instructions are also emailed to you after your appointment, and you receive a printed copy to take home.
  • Contact our office with any concerns after treatment.
  • Most hyaluronic acid fillers are temporary. Katherine reviews expected timing for your plan.
