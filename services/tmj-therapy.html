What it is

How TMJ therapy works

The temporomandibular joint (TMJ) connects your jaw to your skull. When the jaw muscles stay tense, they can cause soreness, clicking, and headaches.

Katherine reviews your symptoms first, then recommends a plan. Options can include massage and other conservative care, along with masseter treatment, which uses a neuromodulator to relax the jaw muscle.

Who it helps
Adults with jaw tension, soreness, clicking, or grinding that affects daily comfort.

Common questions

Questions about TMJ therapy

What is TMJ?

The temporomandibular joint connects your jaw to your skull. Tension in the jaw muscles can cause soreness, clicking, and headaches.

Is masseter treatment FDA-approved for TMJ?

Neuromodulator products are FDA-approved for other uses. Using them to relax the masseter muscle for TMJ symptoms is off-label. Katherine explains the current evidence, the benefits, and the limits before you decide.

Will my jaw look different?

Some people notice a softer jaw line as the muscle relaxes. Katherine reviews the expected changes for your face during your consultation.

How long do results last?

Effects are temporary, and timing varies by person. Katherine reviews your plan at follow-up.

Can I use my HSA?

HSA cards are accepted for qualifying treatments when your provider prescribes them for a medical condition. Katherine can explain the documentation your HSA administrator may need.
