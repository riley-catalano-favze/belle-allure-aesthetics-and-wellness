What it is

How wrinkle treatments work

Neuromodulator treatments are injected in small amounts into targeted muscles. The treated muscle relaxes, and the skin above it can look smoother.

Several neuromodulator products are FDA-approved for specific areas, such as frown lines between the brows and crow's feet. Katherine confirms the product she uses, the areas she recommends, and any off-label use before you decide.

Who it helps
Adults who want softer expression lines with natural-looking movement.
Your visit
A consultation to map your goals, then a brief treatment with a fine needle.
Results
Changes often begin within several days and continue to develop over about two weeks.
Duration
Results typically last several months. Timing varies by person and treatment area.

Common questions

Questions about Wrinkle treatments

How many units will I need?

The number of units depends on the areas you choose and your goals. Katherine lists the units for each area in your plan after your consultation, and the price is confirmed then.

How soon will I see results?

Changes often begin within several days. Full results develop over about two weeks, and a follow-up visit reviews your progress.

How long do results last?

Results typically last several months. Timing varies by person, product, and treatment area.

Will my expressions still look natural?

Katherine tailors each plan so your expressions look natural and balanced.

Is there downtime?

Most people return to daily activities right away. Katherine reviews aftercare steps with you before you leave.

Who should have a health review first?

Katherine reviews your history before treatment. A different option or a short delay may be recommended for pregnancy or breastfeeding, neuromuscular conditions, or allergies to ingredients.

Important information

About results

  • Results are personal. They depend on your health history, the treatment area, and how your body responds.
  • Client stories reflect one person's experience and are not a guarantee of results.
  • Katherine Clark, RN, works under the oversight of a licensed medical doctor.
  • Some treatment areas, such as lip lines and the lower face, are off-label uses. Katherine explains the approved uses of each product and any off-label use before treatment.

Before treatment

  • Katherine reviews your health history and current medications before any treatment.
  • Bring a current list of your medications and supplements to your consultation.
  • You sign informed consent in person with Katherine before treatment begins.

After treatment

  • Katherine reviews written aftercare instructions with you before you leave the office.
  • Your aftercare instructions are also emailed to you after your appointment, and you receive a printed copy to take home.
  • Contact our office with any concerns after treatment.
  • Temporary bruising, redness, or a small bump at the injection site can occur and usually settles within a few days.

Photos and pricing

  • All visits are by appointment. A card is kept on file to hold your appointment.
  • Services, hours, and pricing may change. Call 574-735-1528 or book an appointment online to confirm current availability.
  • Pricing starts as low as $10 per unit. Katherine confirms the total for your plan during your consultation.
