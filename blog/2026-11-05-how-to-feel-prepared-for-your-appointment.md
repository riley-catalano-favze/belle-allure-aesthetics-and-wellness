Walking into a clinic for the first time can feel unfamiliar, even when you are excited about your goals. The good news is that a little preparation goes a long way. At Belle Allure, your first visit is a conversation, not a sales pitch. Katherine Clark, RN, will take time to understand what you want, review your health history, and explain your options in plain language. This guide will help you arrive feeling organized and confident.

## Start with your goals

Before your visit, spend a few minutes thinking about what you want to change or improve. Maybe you want softer lines around your eyes, more volume in your lips, help with a skin concern, or support for your overall wellness. You do not need a perfect answer. Writing down two or three goals, and noting what bothers you most, gives your consultation a clear starting point.

It also helps to think about what matters to you beyond the results, such as how much downtime you can take, how often you want to come in, and what kind of budget you have in mind. Sharing these details early helps us suggest options that fit your life.

## Gather your health information

Your health history is one of the most important parts of your consultation. Before your appointment, make a simple list of:

- Current prescription medications, over-the-counter medicines, and supplements, including doses if you know them
- Any allergies, especially to medications, latex, or anesthetics
- Past cosmetic or medical procedures, including the date and the provider, if you remember
- Any past reactions to treatments, even mild ones
- Any conditions your primary care provider has diagnosed

You can complete our intake forms ahead of time, or bring this list with you. Having it written down means you will not have to rely on memory while you are in the room.

## Ask about medications before your visit

Some medications and supplements can affect how your skin responds to a treatment or how you bruise. Please do not stop a prescribed medication on your own. Instead, ask us during scheduling or your consultation whether anything on your list should be adjusted before a treatment. Our team will review your history and let you know what, if anything, you should change and when.

## Write down your questions

It is easy to forget questions once you are sitting in the consultation room. Writing them down in advance helps you stay focused. Useful questions include:

- What do you recommend for my goals, and why?
- What are the other options, including doing nothing?
- What should I expect during and after the treatment?
- How long do results usually last?
- What are the common side effects, and when should I call the clinic?
- How will we track my progress over time?

You can also review our [frequently asked questions](https://belleallureaestheticsandwellness.com/faq) to see whether your questions are already answered there.

## Plan your day

Arrive a few minutes early so you have time to check in and complete any paperwork. If you plan to use a health savings account (HSA) card, bring it with you. Wear comfortable clothing that gives easy access to the area you want to discuss, and avoid wearing makeup to the consultation if your treatment area is your face, so it is easy for us to see your skin clearly.

If you are coming for a treatment rather than a consultation, plan to give yourself time afterward to relax. Some people feel fine right away, while others prefer a quiet evening.

## Know what your first visit includes

Your first consultation with a registered nurse at our Elkhart clinic is designed to help you make an informed decision. You can expect a review of your goals and health history, an honest discussion of treatment options and what results are realistic, and time to ask questions. You are never required to book a treatment on the same day, and you are welcome to take information home and think it over.

Our treatments are overseen by a licensed medical doctor, and we will explain any part of your care that you do not understand. If you learn something new about your health during the visit, we will talk through what it means for your plan.

## Plan for after your visit

Before you leave, make sure you have written aftercare instructions and know how to reach us with questions. If a follow-up visit is recommended, you can schedule it at the front desk or online. Keeping notes about how you feel and what you notice can also make your next appointment more productive.
