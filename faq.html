How our practice works

Every visit follows the same clear path

Your plan is built around your goals before any treatment is recommended. Open each step to see what happens.

1Consultation

Your goals, skin or hair history, and current medications are reviewed. Katherine Clark, RN, listens first and asks questions to understand what matters most to you.

Every plan begins here, including for first-time clients.

Answers before your first visit

Booking and your first visit

Do I need a consultation before a treatment?

Every plan begins with a consultation where your goals, skin or hair history, and current medications are reviewed. Your provider then explains which options fit your needs.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a list of current medications, past treatments, and any questions. Complete your consent and health history forms online before your visit, or on arrival. Either way, we are ready for you.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the online scheduler at belleallurefacialaesthetics.myaestheticrecord.com, or call 574-735-1528 during office hours. Staff can help you find a time that fits your schedule.

Can I book a consultation before choosing a treatment?

Yes. Book a consultation to talk through your goals. Your provider will recommend the options that fit your needs.

How far in advance should I book?

Book as early as you can to secure the time that works best for you. Call 574-735-1528 if you need help finding an opening.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes. Call 574-735-1528 during office hours to move your visit to a new time.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

Call 574-735-1528 during office hours, and our team will walk you through our current policy and help you find a new time.

Medical oversight and safety

What is a physician-owned medical practice?

A physician-owned medical practice is a clinic that is owned and led by a licensed physician. At Belle Allure, an MD serving as Medical Director holds the practice's medical license and oversees every treatment plan. Katherine Clark, RN, provides your consultations and treatments under that medical oversight.

Who oversees my care?

An MD serves as the Medical Director of Belle Allure Aesthetics and Wellness in Elkhart, Indiana. Katherine's care is overseen by that MD, and the practice operates under the MD's medical license.

Who performs my treatments?

Katherine Clark, RN, leads consultations and treatments with 20+ years of medical experience. She is overseen by a medical doctor.

Which weight loss medications does Belle Allure offer?

GLP-1 weight loss medications are prescribed after a medical evaluation, with ongoing guidance from your provider.

Will my treatment be explained before it starts?

Yes. Each step is explained before it happens, and you can ask questions or request a pause at any point during your visit.

What happens after my treatment?

You receive aftercare instructions and scheduled check-ins with your care team, so your provider can see how you are doing and adjust your plan.

Can I ask questions about my medications?

Yes. Bring your full medication list to your consultation. Your provider reviews it as part of your medical history.

Payment and HSA cards

When do I pay for my treatment?

You pay after your visit. We keep a card on file before your appointment, so you can focus on your care.

Can I use my HSA for treatments?

HSA is accepted for qualifying medical treatments, including TMJ Therapy, headache treatment, and IV therapy when your provider prescribes it for a medical condition. Cosmetic treatments such as wrinkle treatments and dermal fillers are paid for directly.

Which treatments can I pay for with an HSA?

TMJ Therapy and headache treatment qualify. IV therapy qualifies when your provider prescribes it for a medical condition.

Which treatments are paid for directly?

Cosmetic treatments such as wrinkle treatments and dermal fillers are paid for directly at your visit.

Do you offer payment plans or financing?

HSA cards are accepted for qualifying treatments, including TMJ therapy, headache treatment, and IV therapy when your provider prescribes it for a medical condition. For other payment options, call 574-735-1528 to ask about what is available for your treatment.

Results and aftercare

How soon will I see results?

Results vary by service. Some treatments show changes within days, and others build over several weeks. Your provider will explain what to expect for your plan.

How long do results last?

Duration depends on the treatment, your body, and your goals. Your provider will review expected timing for your plan and check in with you during follow-up visits.

Will I need repeat treatments, and how often?

Many services are maintenance-based, and the timing depends on the treatment and your goals. Your provider will recommend a schedule during your consultation.

Is there downtime after treatment?

Many services fit into a regular schedule with minimal downtime. Your provider will share any aftercare steps during your visit.

Can I drive myself home after my visit?

Most visits allow you to drive yourself home. If a service calls for a different plan, your provider will tell you before your treatment begins.

Do prices change?

Our prices stay the same. Discounts are offered from time to time, so call 574-735-1528 to ask about current offers.

Which payment processor do you use?

Payments are processed securely through Stripe.
